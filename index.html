Welcome to Makati's Hidden Cafe!

Discover a cozy spot for coffee and conversation.



Majikasa Cafe

Majikasa is a hidden café along Chino Roces known for its cozy, artistic, and eclectic interior. It has lots of plants, stylish furniture, and a relaxing atmosphere.

A great choice for people who want a quiet, aesthetic, and unique café experience—perfect for studying, working, taking photos, or simply relaxing.




Saglit Cafe

Saglit Café is a cozy, artsy café that combines coffee and flowers, offering artisan drinks and unique mocktails in a charming, creative space.

Perfect for those looking for a peaceful and Instagram-worthy place to enjoy coffee, appreciate flowers, and take a short break from a busy day.




Alô Café

Alô Café is a vibrant Vietnamese-inspired café in Makati, serving drinks such as Vietnamese coffee, egg coffee, and salted coffee, alongside Vietnamese dishes.

A good choice for anyone who wants to try something different, especially authentic Vietnamese-style coffee and food in a chill, modern setting.
